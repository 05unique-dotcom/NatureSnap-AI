# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in NatureSnap AI a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, threats, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or restrict participation when this code is violated. Report concerns privately using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when they involve a safety or privacy risk; otherwise contact the maintainers through the repository.

This project follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
